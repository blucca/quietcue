Fix the camera in place. Keep your face and chest visible, well lit, and still during the quiet stages.

Each SDK session automatically sends derived measurements and session/request metadata to Presage. Presage sends measurements to Anthropic to generate AI Insights.

Camera & data details

Camera images are processed on this device. Insights requests contain derived measurements and metadata; images, video, audio and face data stay outside those requests. QuietCue supplies zero script or prompt text to the SDK.

Automatic Insights processing stays on for each SDK session. Diagnostic telemetry is disabled.

Presage stores Insights requests and responses while the developer account is active and deletes them within 60 days after it closes. Backups expire within 90 days. Anthropic’s commercial terms provide limited retention and exclude model training on customer information.

For a local video, use a recording you have permission to process, showing a consenting adult. The file is configured on the local server with QUIETCUE_VIDEO_PATH.
