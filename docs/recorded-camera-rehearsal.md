## Record one continuous clip

- Use a stable camera, ordinary 30-fps MP4, and good light on your face and
  upper chest. A 720p recording is suitable for this first task.
- Stay still and quiet for **45 seconds**, breathing naturally. QuietCue needs
  a valid 30-second quiet window with usable confidence; this opening leaves
  time for SDK acquisition.
- Read your own paragraph for **20–30 seconds**. Pause about two seconds
  partway through, then continue.
- Stay still and quiet for another **45 seconds**. Keep the clip continuous.

We use the input's SDK clock and actual readiness state when operating the
replay. The returned notes show the measured result and the step reached.
After viewing them, answer: **Did this replay help you review your own pause?
Yes / No; what would you change?**

## Data and deletion

Source video and face-derived features are processed by SmartSpectra on our
computer. Every SDK session automatically sends derived measurements and
session/request metadata to Presage in the US, which sends measurements to
Anthropic for Insights. QuietCue supplies zero prompt text to the SDK. Provider
retention and deletion follow the
[SmartSpectra privacy policy](https://presagetech.com/legal/privacy-policy).

We keep received task files private for feedback, demo preparation and
verification, then delete our copies when challenge follow-up ends, by
**2027-01-31**. Request earlier deletion in the original reply thread. The
private transfer method has its own data handling, which we agree before
receipt. The proposed public cut shows the app; source face and audio stay
private. Public use of the source video requires a separate written grant.

For a live run on your own computer, use the existing
[local camera setup](../README.md#run-on-your-computer) and the app's camera/data
consent screen.
